Our research into hydrogels focuses on the fundamental interplay between chemical architecture, hydration, and macroscopic mechanical response.

### Research Themes
* **Stimuli-Responsive Behavior:** Modeling how temperature, pH, and light drive massive volume changes and phase transitions.
* **Statistical Mechanics Frameworks:** Deriving constitutive laws that account for the dissociation of hydrogen bonds and molecular-level network evolution.
* **Bio-Hybrid Systems:** Investigating the interaction between synthetic gels and biological tissues for medical applications.
